ABOUT US • THE TIMBER LIGHTS

Architectural Focal Point. Warm, Balanced Illumination.

The Timber Lights was founded with a clear vision — to create statement pendant lighting that feels architectural, timeless, and uncompromising in quality.

By combining material craftsmanship with advanced lighting technology, we have developed refined wooden pendant systems that integrate seamlessly into sophisticated environments.

• Clean, disciplined design

• Premium, carefully selected materials (Ghana Teak Wood)

• Precision engineering and detailing

• Warm, balanced illumination

Lighting is not an accessory. It is an architectural focal point.

“In every space, there is a light that holds attention. We choose to illuminate it.”

— The Timber Lights Philosophy
Our Commitment to Excellence: Dedicated to using sustainable materials, creating timeless designs, and showcasing artisanal craftsmanship.
